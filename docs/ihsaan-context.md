## Supplied narrative

At IHSAAN, our mission is to empower every Muslim student and young professional with opportunities, resources, and networks to unlock their dream careers through which they can build a more God-conscious and ethical society.
Government reports have stated that “Muslims experience the greatest economic disadvantages of any group in UK society. Muslims are excluded, discriminated against, or failed, at all stages of their transition from education to employment” (Social Mobility Commission 2017).
We’re building a better future through community, mentorship, faith-rooted career guides, exclusive career opportunities, and events that connect, inform and inspire.
Your role in this journey is vital. Every person who joins our team contributes to the collective effort of empowering the next generation of Muslim professionals to rise, serve, and lead with purpose.

What is IHSAAN?
IHSAAN is a grassroots, career-oriented organisation dedicated to empowering Muslim students and graduates. Our goal is to ensure that every Muslim student and recent graduate in the UK and Ireland has access to the tools, support, and opportunities needed to pursue successful, fulfilling careers that align with their faith.
By bridging the gap between university and professional life, we help young Muslims enter their dream career paths - while nurturing a community of profession.
IHSAAN operates within FOSIS as its dedicated careers and professional development division. It is led by the Head of Careers, who runs IHSAAN and sits on the FOSIS National Executive Committee, ensuring that IHSAAN’s work is aligned with FOSIS’ wider strategy and student-focused mission. Through this structure, IHSAAN delivers career initiatives while benefiting from FOSIS’ national network, governance, and organisational support.


Our Values
At IHSAAN, everything we do is rooted in our faith. Our name itself - Ihsaan -  reminds us to strive for excellence in both intention and action, seeking Allah’s pleasure in every effort.
Our work is not simply about career development - it’s about nurturing Muslims who embody faith, character, and service in all areas of life.
We are guided by the words of the Prophet ﷺ:
“Whoever relieves a Muslim of some worldly distress, Allah will relieve him of some of the distress of the Day of Resurrection; and whoever conceals the faults of a Muslim, Allah will conceal his faults in this world and the Hereafter.
 Allah helps His servant so long as His servant helps his brother.
 Whoever follows a path in pursuit of knowledge, Allah will make easy for him a path to Paradise...”
 (Sunan Ibn Majah, 225)
This hadith captures the essence of our mission - service, sincerity, and the pursuit of knowledge. By helping others rise, Allah will also grant barakah (divine blessing) to our own journeys.
Our Core Values
Excellence - Ihsaan - We perform to our utmost ability in all that we do, ensuring excellence in our standard and quality of work.

God-Consciousness - Taqwa - We make deliberate and careful decisions with awareness of our accountability to Allah and to our community.

Ambition - Himmah (change) - We invest in our community, bringing soft skills, leadership and professional development to create a more ethical and just society.

Trust - Amanah - We uphold our integrity through transparency and maintain ethical decision making in our processes.

Service - Khidmah - We treat everyone with respect, compassion and care, and ensure our work supports the people we aim to serve.
